Support

Orbit Arena · satisfying physics sims you can play

Get the iOS app on the App Store, or play on the web.

How do I play?

Before each sim starts, pick who you think wins (or guess over/under). Then watch: every bounce plays the next note of a public-domain melody. A correct pick earns points and grows your streak. Points are only a score.

My extras disappeared

Open the menu, tap See extras, then Restore purchases. Extras belong to the Apple ID (iOS) or Google account (Android) that bought them, so sign in with that account on the new device. Purchases don't carry over between iOS and Android.

A purchase says it's waiting

If Ask to Buy (iOS) or purchase approvals (Android) are on, a parent or guardian has to approve the purchase. On Android a payment can also wait while it clears, for example cash at a store. It unlocks by itself once it's done.

Refunds

Purchases go through the store, so the store handles refunds. iOS: visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID. Android: follow Google Play's refund steps.

Sound doesn't play

Tap the screen once (browsers wait for a tap before playing sound), and check the speaker button at the top.

Flashing and fast motion

Sims have fast motion and flashing colors. Turn on Reduced motion in the menu for fewer particles and shorter trails. If you or your child has epilepsy or has had a seizure, talk to a doctor before playing, and stop if you feel unwell (see the health note in the terms).
