How do I play?

Before each sim starts, pick who you think wins (or guess over/under). Then watch: every bounce plays the next note of a public-domain melody. A correct pick earns points and grows your streak. Points are only a score.

My extras disappeared (iOS)

Open the menu, tap See extras, then Restore purchases. Extras belong to the Apple ID that bought them, so sign in with that Apple ID on the new device.

A purchase says it's waiting

If Ask to Buy is on, a parent or guardian has to approve the purchase. It unlocks by itself once it's approved.

Refunds

Purchases go through Apple, so Apple handles refunds: visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.

Sound doesn't play

Tap the screen once (browsers wait for a tap before playing sound), and check the speaker button at the top.

Flashing and fast motion

Sims have fast motion and flashing colors. Turn on Reduced motion in the menu for fewer particles and shorter trails. If you or your child has epilepsy or has had a seizure, talk to a doctor before playing, and stop if you feel unwell (see the health note in the terms).
