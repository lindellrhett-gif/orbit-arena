Privacy policy

Orbit Arena · last updated October 28, 2026

The short version

Orbit Arena doesn't collect personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your points, streak, pick history for the daily sims, and settings (sound, reduced motion) are saved on your device only. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app (or clearing this site's data) deletes them.

Share links

When you tap Share, the app makes a link that describes the sim (its type, colors or countries, and a number called the seed). It contains nothing about you. You choose where to send it.

Children

Orbit Arena is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Points

Points are only a score. They can't be bought, sold, spent or exchanged for anything.

Changes

If we ever add something that handles data (for example, ads or anonymous usage statistics), we'll update this page first and say exactly what is collected.
