Privacy policy

Orbit Arena · last updated November 6, 2026

The short version

Orbit Arena doesn't collect personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your points, streak, pick history for the daily sims, saved sims, and settings (sound, reduced motion, ball skin and trail) are saved on your device only. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app (or clearing this site's data) deletes them.

There are no cookies. The website keeps your game data in your browser's local storage only so the game can remember it; nothing in it identifies you, and it never leaves your device.

Hosting and app stores

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address to keep its service running and secure; we don't receive or use them (see GitHub's privacy statement). The iOS app is downloaded through Apple, under Apple's privacy policy. The game loads no ads, analytics, fonts or anything else from other companies.

Share links

When you tap Share, the app makes a link that describes the sim (its type, colors or countries, and a number called the seed). It contains nothing about you. You choose where to send it.

In-app purchases (iOS app)

Extras in the iOS app are one-time purchases made through Apple. Apple handles the payment and your Apple ID; we never see your name, email or payment details. The app only asks Apple which extras this Apple ID owns, and remembers that on your device so they work offline. Restore Purchases asks Apple again.

Children

Orbit Arena is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children. On iOS, Apple's Ask to Buy lets a parent approve any purchase.

Points

Points are only a score. They can't be bought, sold, spent or exchanged for anything.

Your rights and contacting us

Because we don't collect personal data, there's nothing about you for us to access, correct, export or delete. If you contact us (see the support page), we use your message only to reply, and the service you write through (GitHub or Instagram) handles it under its own policy. If you submit a sim with a credit name, that name may be posted with your sim (see the terms), so please use a nickname.

Changes

If we ever add something that handles data (for example, ads or anonymous usage statistics), we'll update this page first and say exactly what is collected.
